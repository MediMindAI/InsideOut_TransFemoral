## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

### Company: **NEXUS VASCULAR**

### Device: **Apex™ Inside-out Access & Closure System**

**Full Device Name:** Nexus Vascular Apex™ Inside-out Access & Closure System

**Marketing Name:** Apex™ System

---

### Brand Essence

**Nexus Vascular** represents the critical connection point where innovation meets clinical excellence—uniting transradial and femoral access, connecting inside-out technology with outside success, linking access with closure in a single device.

**Apex™** positions this technology at the pinnacle of vascular access evolution—the highest point of precision, performance, and patient safety.

---

### Core Innovation

A catheter-based system that establishes retrograde femoral artery access from a transradial approach while simultaneously deploying a pre-sutured closure mechanism via proprietary "Pentad-Needle Array" (5-port system).

**Target Market:** Large-bore interventional procedures (TAVR, EVAR, Impella, complex PCI) where access complications represent 30-50% of procedural morbidity.

**Category:** Hybrid Access/Closure Systems (category-creating)

**Market Size:** $2.1B global vascular closure device market growing at 8.4% CAGR

- Use Merriweather for clinical publications, case studies
- Maintain 1.5-1.6 line-height for body text (readability)

## BRAND GUIDELINES

### Brand DO's

**Visual Identity:**
- ✅ Use Nexus Deep Blue (#0B3D91) as primary brand color for all major branding
- ✅ Use Apex Electric Cyan (#00E0FF) as innovation accent, highlights, CTAs
- ✅ Use Connection Teal (#00B4D8) as secondary accent for connection-themed content
- ✅ Maintain generous white space in all layouts—clean, uncluttered
- ✅ Use Connection Node logo as primary lockup (icon + wordmark)
- ✅ Use Montserrat for headlines, Inter for body text, Merriweather for clinical publications

**Messaging:**
- ✅ Always lead with "Where Access Meets Closure" tagline in marketing materials
- ✅ Emphasize "inside-out" and "prophylactic closure" as key differentiators
- ✅ Use clinical language with physicians, outcome/ROI language with administrators
- ✅ Reference "Nexus" as connection point and "Apex" as peak performance
- ✅ Highlight hybrid device nature (not just closure)

**Tone:**
- ✅ Professional, confident, authoritative
- ✅ Clinical credibility with innovation forward-thinking
- ✅ Serious about patient safety, bold about technology
- ✅ Respectful of competitors but clear about differentiation

**Applications:**
- ✅ Use high-quality 3D device renders (not low-res photos)
- ✅ Use clean medical illustration style for procedure animations
- ✅ Ensure all materials pass WCAG AA accessibility standards (color contrast)
- ✅ Test all designs in grayscale to ensure they work without color

---

### Brand DON'Ts

**Visual Identity:**
- ❌ DON'T use red as primary color (competitive space, avoid confusion)
- ❌ DON'T use busy patterns or textures (keep it clean, professional)
- ❌ DON'T stretch or distort logo (maintain proportions always)
- ❌ DON'T place logo on low-contrast backgrounds (must be clearly visible)
- ❌ DON'T use more than 3 typefaces in a single piece
- ❌ DON'T use neon or overly bright colors beyond Apex Electric Cyan accent

**Messaging:**
- ❌ DON'T compare directly to Abbott/Terumo by name in public materials (above-the-fray positioning)
- ❌ DON'T use overly aggressive language ("destroy the competition", etc.)
- ❌ DON'T claim outcomes before clinical data supports (regulatory risk)
- ❌ DON'T use "revolutionary" excessively (once per piece max)
- ❌ DON'T oversimplify the technology to point of inaccuracy

**Tone:**
- ❌ DON'T be arrogant or dismissive of current standard of care
- ❌ DON'T use fear-mongering about complications (factual, not sensational)
- ❌ DON'T be overly technical with non-clinical audiences
- ❌ DON'T use humor in clinical contexts (serious procedures, serious tone)

**Applications:**
- ❌ DON'T use stock photography of doctors/patients (custom photography or medical illustrations only)
- ❌ DON'T use consumer-style marketing (this is B2B medical device)
- ❌ DON'T over-design or use trendy effects that will date quickly
- ❌ DON'T create materials without checking brand guidelines first

---

### Logo Usage Guidelines

**Clear Space:**
- Minimum clear space around logo = height of "N" in "NEXUS"
- No text, graphics, or page edges within clear space

**Minimum Size:**
- Print: 0.5 inches wide (for full horizontal lockup)
- Digital: 120px wide (for horizontal lockup)
- Icon only: 32px minimum (favicons, app icons)

**Backgrounds:**
- **Preferred:** White or very light backgrounds (logo in Nexus Deep Blue + Cyan)
- **Dark Backgrounds:** Use white version of logo
- **Colored Backgrounds:** Ensure sufficient contrast (test in grayscale)
- **Photos:** Place logo on solid color panel/overlay, not directly on busy photo

**Incorrect Usage:**
```
❌ DON'T rotate logo at angles
❌ DON'T apply drop shadows or effects
❌ DON'T change colors (only approved color variations allowed)
❌ DON'T rearrange elements (icon and wordmark positioning is fixed)
❌ DON'T place on low-contrast background
❌ DON'T outline logo (it already has strong form)
```

---

### Color Accessibility

**Contrast Ratios (WCAG AA Compliance):**

Text combinations that pass:
- ✅ Nexus Deep Blue (#0B3D91) on White (#FFFFFF): 10.2:1 (AAA)
- ✅ Charcoal (#1A202C) on White: 14.8:1 (AAA)
- ✅ White on Nexus Deep Blue: 10.2:1 (AAA)
- ✅ White on Graphite Navy (#1B2B3A): 12.1:1 (AAA)

Combinations to AVOID:
- ❌ Apex Electric Cyan (#00E0FF) on White: 2.9:1 (FAIL - use for accents only, not body text)
- ❌ Connection Teal (#00B4D8) on White: 3.1:1 (FAIL - use for accents only)
- ❌ Silver Gray (#A0AEC0) on White: 2.4:1 (FAIL - use for de-emphasized text only, larger sizes)

**Solution:**
- Use Nexus Deep Blue or Charcoal for ALL body text
- Use Cyan/Teal ONLY for accents, buttons, icons, divider lines
- Ensure text on Cyan/Teal buttons is white or very dark for contrast
