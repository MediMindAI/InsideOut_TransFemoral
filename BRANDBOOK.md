## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This brandbook presents comprehensive branding strategies for a groundbreaking hybrid vascular access/closure device that transforms femoral artery access from an uncertain manual procedure into a reproducible, device-mediated solution with prophylactic closure capability.

**Core Innovation:** Inside-out retrograde femoral access via transradial approach with integrated pre-sutured closure system featuring proprietary "Pentad-Needle Array" (5-port system).

**Target Market:** High-risk large-bore interventional procedures (TAVR, EVAR, Impella, complex PCI) where access complications drive 30-50% of procedural morbidity.

**Brand Strategy Focus:** Communicate precision, innovation, and reliability while establishing category leadership in hybrid access/closure systems.

## BRAND MESSAGING & POSITIONING

### For SENTINEL VASCULAR | ProGuard™ System

#### Brand Positioning Statement

*"Sentinel Vascular is the guardian of vascular access, pioneering inside-out retrograde technology that transforms large-bore femoral procedures from high-risk manual skill into reproducible, device-mediated precision with prophylactic closure—protecting patients, clinicians, and outcomes."*

---

#### Core Messages

**Message Pillar 1: Precision from Within**
- Traditional "outside-in" femoral access is blind and variable
- Sentinel's inside-out approach uses the vessel lumen as a roadmap
- Fluoroscopic targeting eliminates guesswork and "rolling artery" complications
- **Proof Point:** Exact placement at intended CFA location, every time

**Message Pillar 2: Prophylactic Protection**
- Closure devices can fail when deployed after sheath removal
- ProGuard™ deploys sutures *before* large-bore access—protection from the start
- Pre-positioned sutures eliminate end-of-case device failure risk
- **Proof Point:** Sutures in place before arteriotomy, guaranteed hemostasis

**Message Pillar 3: Access Complexity Solved**
- Obesity, scarring, tortuosity, calcification make traditional access treacherous
- Navigate from inside, bypass anatomical challenges
- Transradial approach eliminates femoral access site complications
- **Proof Point:** Access success regardless of BMI or vascular anatomy

---

#### Key Value Propositions

**For Interventional Cardiologists:**
- "Stop fighting blind sticks and failed closures"
- Reproducible access technique—skill-agnostic procedure
- Confidence in large-bore TAVR, Impella, complex PCI

**For Hospital Administrators:**
- Reduce vascular complication costs ($8,000-$45,000 per complication)
- Shorter procedure times, faster patient recovery
- Differentiated structural heart program capability

**For Patients:**
- Minimized access site complications (bleeding, hematoma, pseudoaneurysm)
- Faster recovery, reduced hospital stay
- Radial artery preference (awake, comfortable, walk sooner)

**For Investors/VCs:**
- **TAM:** $2.1B global vascular closure device market (growing 8.4% CAGR)
- **Category Creation:** Hybrid access/closure—no direct competitors
- **IP Moat:** Pentad-needle array, inside-out retrograde methodology, pre-sutured closure
- **Regulatory Path:** 510(k) likely (predicate: closure devices + access needles)
- **Clinical Problem:** 30-50% of TAVR complications are access-related—massive unmet need
- **Margin Profile:** Premium device pricing, single-use, high-ASP ($800-$1,500 estimated)

---

#### Tagline Recommendations

**Primary Tagline:** *"Precision from Within"*
- Simple, memorable, technically accurate
- Captures inside-out mechanism and exact targeting benefit

**Alternative Taglines:**
- *"Access Perfected"* (bold claim, outcome-focused)
- *"The Inside Advantage"* (competitive differentiation)
- *"Guarding Every Access"* (brand integration, protective theme)

---

#### Messaging Hierarchy

**Headline/Hero Statement:**
*"Inside-Out Innovation, Outside Excellence"*

**Subhead:**
*"Sentinel Vascular ProGuard™: The first hybrid access and closure system that navigates from within to deliver precise femoral access with pre-positioned suture closure."*

**Body Copy Template:**
*"Traditional femoral access is a manual skill dependent on palpation, guesswork, and luck. Sentinel Vascular's ProGuard™ System eliminates uncertainty. Using a transradial approach, our catheter navigates the aorta to reach the femoral artery from inside—using the vessel lumen itself as the guide. At the precise fluoroscopic target, our proprietary Pentad-Needle Array deploys: one central access needle for your large-bore sheath, surrounded by four pre-positioned suture needles. Your closure is in place before you even begin the case. No more blind sticks. No failed closures. Just precision, protection, and peace of mind."*

## TYPOGRAPHY STANDARDS

### For SENTINEL VASCULAR (Professional Precision Identity)

#### Primary Typeface: **Inter** (Sans-Serif)

**Logo & Headlines:** Inter Bold (700)
- Clean, modern, highly legible
- Professional without being cold
- Excellent at small sizes (device labels, packaging)

**Body Text:** Inter Regular (400)
- Subheads: Inter Medium (500)
- Captions: Inter Light (300)

**Rationale:**
- Open-source, cost-effective for startup
- Designed for screen readability (website, apps, digital sales tools)
- Professional tech-medical positioning
- Wide language support (global markets)

**Download:** https://rsms.me/inter/

---

#### Secondary Typeface: **Crimson Pro** (Serif)

**Use Cases:**
- Long-form clinical white papers
- Case studies and publications
- Premium printed materials

**Rationale:**
- Serif adds credibility and gravitas for scientific content
- Pairs beautifully with Inter
- Maintains readability in dense technical text

---

#### Sizing Guidelines

**Digital/Screen:**
- Hero Headlines: 48-64px (Inter Bold)
- Section Headers: 32-40px (Inter Bold)
- Subheads: 20-24px (Inter Medium)
- Body Text: 16-18px (Inter Regular)
- Captions: 14px (Inter Regular)

**Print:**
- Poster/Banner Headlines: 72-120pt (Inter Bold)
- Brochure Headers: 24-36pt (Inter Bold)
- Body Text: 10-12pt (Inter Regular)
- Fine Print: 8pt (Inter Light)
