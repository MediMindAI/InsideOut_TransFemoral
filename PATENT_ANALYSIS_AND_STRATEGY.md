## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Your device represents a **first-in-class hybrid access/closure system** combining three novel elements into a single platform:

1. **Transradial-to-Femoral Navigation** - Retrograde intraluminal approach
2. **Inside-Out Arteriotomy Creation** - Fluoroscopy-guided, precision puncture from within the vessel
3. **Prophylactic Pre-Suture Deployment** - Closure mechanism placed BEFORE large-bore sheath insertion

### Patentability Assessment: **STRONG NOVELTY POTENTIAL**

**Key Finding:** No existing patents identified that combine all three core elements of your device. This represents a genuine innovation gap in the vascular closure device market.

### Market Opportunity: **HIGH VALUE**

- Global vascular closure device market: **$1.77B (2025) → $2.47B (2030)** at 6.98% CAGR
- Large-bore segment (≥12F): **8.12% CAGR** driven by TAVR/EVAR growth
- TAVR procedures approaching **300,000 annually** worldwide
- Access complications remain at **12-22%** despite device improvements
- Critical unmet need for predictable hemostasis in large-bore (up to 25F) access

**Critical Gap Identified:** No patents combine transradial navigation WITH femoral inside-out puncture AND integrated closure.

**Your Innovation:** Sutures deployed FROM INSIDE before external sheath even exists.

### 2.2 International Protection Strategy (PCT Route)

#### Recommended Path: **Provisional → PCT → National Phase**

**Why PCT for Medical Devices:**
1. **Time Advantage:** Delays national filing costs up to 30-31 months
2. **Market Assessment Window:** Time to gather clinical data, refine product, seek partnerships
3. **Funding Alignment:** Time to secure Series A funding before expensive national phases
4. **Regulatory Coordination:** Align patent prosecution with FDA/CE Mark progress
5. **Geographic Flexibility:** Choose final markets after viability proven

#### PCT Timeline & Strategy:

```
Month 0: File PROVISIONAL Patent Application (USA or Georgia)
         ↓ Establishes priority date
         ↓ Cost: $2,000-$5,000
         ↓ Allows 12 months refinement
         ↓
Month 12: File PCT International Application
          ↓ Claims priority from provisional
          ↓ Cost: $4,000-$8,000 (filing) + $3,000-$7,000 (attorney)
          ↓ Receives international search report (ISR)
          ↓
Month 18: PCT Publication
          ↓ Public disclosure of application
          ↓ "Patent Pending" status globally
          ↓
Month 30-31: National Phase Entry Deadline
             ↓ Select target countries
             ↓ Enter national/regional offices
             ↓
Months 36-60: National Prosecution
              ↓ Examination, office actions, amendments
              ↓
Months 48-84: Patent Grants
              (varies by country)
```

## PART 4: REGULATORY PATHWAY

### 4.1 FDA Strategy (United States)

#### Device Classification:

**Likely Classification:** Class II - Moderate Risk
**Regulatory Route:** 510(k) Premarket Notification
**Predicate Device:** Perclose ProGlide Suture-Mediated Closure System (P960043)

**Rationale:**
- Similar intended use (femoral artery closure)
- Similar technology (suture-mediated)
- Same patient population (interventional cardiology/radiology)
- Same anatomical site (common femoral artery)

**Alternative:** If FDA considers inside-out approach substantially different:
- **Class III** - High Risk
- **Regulatory Route:** De Novo or PMA (Premarket Approval)
- Timeline: 3-5 years (vs. 18-24 months for 510(k))
- Cost: $2M-$5M (vs. $500K-$1M for 510(k))

#### Recommended FDA Engagement Strategy:

**Month 6-12:**
✅ **Pre-Submission Meeting (Q-Sub)**
- Present device concept to FDA
- Discuss classification (Class II vs. III)
- Confirm 510(k) pathway viability
- Discuss testing requirements (bench, animal, clinical)
- Cost: $5,000-$15,000 (attorney prep)

**Month 12-24:**
✅ **Develop 510(k) Submission Package**
- Design verification testing
- Biocompatibility testing (ISO 10993)
- Sterilization validation
- Shelf-life testing
- Animal studies (porcine femoral access model, n=10-20)
- Clinical data (may use literature for predicate comparison)

**Month 24-30:**
✅ **Submit 510(k)**
- FDA has 90 days to review (often extends to 120-180 days)
- Respond to deficiency letters
- Cost: $500K-$1M total (testing + submission)

**Month 30-36:**
✅ **510(k) Clearance**
- Authorized to market in USA

#### Alternative: PMA Pathway
If FDA requires PMA:
- **Month 12-36:** Preclinical development + animal studies
- **Month 36-48:** IDE (Investigational Device Exemption) submission
- **Month 48-72:** Clinical trial (50-200 patients, multi-center)
- **Month 72-84:** PMA submission
- **Month 84-96:** PMA approval
- **Cost:** $3M-$8M total
