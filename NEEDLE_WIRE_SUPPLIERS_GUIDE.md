## Material Specifications Reference

### Nitinol Wire Standards
- **Standard:** ASTM F2063 - Standard Specification for Wrought Nickel-Titanium Shape Memory Alloys for Medical Devices and Surgical Implants
- **Composition:** Typically 55-56% Nickel, balance Titanium
- **Properties:** Superelastic, shape memory, excellent biocompatibility
- **Common Medical Wire Sizes:** 0.003" to 0.040" (0.076mm to 1.0mm)

### Stainless Steel Wire Standards
- **Grades:** 304, 304V, 316, 316L, 316LVM
- **Standard:** ASTM F138, ASTM F139 (surgical implant grades)
- **Properties:** Corrosion resistant, non-magnetic, excellent strength

### Size Conversions
| French (F) | mm | Inches |
|------------|------|---------|
| 2F | 0.66mm | 0.026" |
| 3F | 1.0mm | 0.039" |
| 4F | 1.33mm | 0.052" |

**Note:** 2F = 0.66mm is your target specification

### Wire Diameter Equivalents
- 0.66mm = 0.026 inches = 2 French
- 0.018 inches = 0.457mm ≈ 1.4F
- 0.025 inches = 0.635mm ≈ 1.9F
- 0.035 inches = 0.889mm ≈ 2.7F

## Procurement Strategy

### Recommended Approach

#### Phase 1: Initial Outreach (Week 1-2)
1. **Priority Tier 1 (Best for Small Batch Testing):**
   - Custom Wire Technologies (USA) - Excellent for startups/small batches
   - Component Supply Company (USA) - Fast shipping, stock items
   - Shenzhen X-Way Medical (China) - Responsive, flexible MOQ

2. **Send simultaneous inquiries** to 3-5 suppliers across different regions
3. **Use Template 1** for general inquiry
4. **Request samples** where possible

#### Phase 2: Evaluation (Week 2-3)
1. Compare responses on:
   - MOQ requirements
   - Pricing (aim for $5-25 per piece for small batches)
   - Lead time
   - Customization capabilities
   - Sample availability

2. **Request samples** from 2-3 most promising suppliers

#### Phase 3: Sample Testing (Week 3-4)
1. Evaluate samples for:
   - Dimensional accuracy
   - Tip sharpness/geometry
   - Material properties
   - Consistency

2. Conduct preliminary perforation tests

#### Phase 4: Order Placement (Week 4-5)
1. Place small initial order (10-20 pieces)
2. Establish relationship with 1-2 reliable suppliers
3. Plan for future orders based on testing needs

### Cost Expectations

**Estimated Pricing (Small Batch Quantities):**
- **China suppliers:** $3-15 per piece (MOQ: 10-50 pieces)
- **USA suppliers:** $10-30 per piece (more flexible on small quantities)
- **European suppliers:** $15-40 per piece (highest quality, strictest standards)

**Additional Costs:**
- Shipping: $50-200 (depending on method and origin)
- Customs/duties: Variable by country
- Sample fees: Often waived or $50-100

### Tips for Successful Procurement

1. **Be Clear About Research Purpose:**
   - Explain this is for testing, not commercial production
   - Some suppliers are more flexible with researchers

2. **Flexibility:**
   - Consider stock sizes close to 0.66mm (e.g., 0.025" = 0.635mm)
   - Adjust length requirements if needed

3. **Documentation:**
   - Request material certificates (especially for nitinol)
   - Ask for technical datasheets
   - Obtain quality documentation

4. **Communication:**
   - For Chinese suppliers: Use WeChat/WhatsApp for faster responses
   - For US/EU suppliers: Email and phone work well
   - Follow up within 3-5 days if no response

5. **Payment:**
   - Small orders: Credit card or PayPal often accepted
   - Larger orders: Wire transfer or purchase order
   - For Chinese suppliers: May request 30-50% deposit

6. **Shipping Considerations:**
   - Medical devices may require special documentation
   - Clearly mark as "Research samples - Not for commercial use"
   - Consider express shipping for small quantities
