## Product Categories Overview

### 1. Drug Eluting Balloons (DEB) / Drug Coated Balloons (DCB)

**Function:** Balloons coated with anti-proliferative drugs (typically Paclitaxel or Sirolimus/Rapamycin) that inhibit smooth muscle cell proliferation and reduce restenosis.

**Key Applications:**
- In-stent restenosis (ISR) treatment
- De novo small vessel disease
- Bifurcation lesions
- Patients unsuitable for stent implantation

**Drug Types:**
| Drug | Mechanism | Key Advantage |
|------|-----------|---------------|
| Paclitaxel | Cytotoxic agent | Most established, extensive clinical data |
| Sirolimus/Rapamycin | Immunosuppressant | Superior safety profile, reduced inflammation |

### 2. Scoring Balloons

**Function:** Balloons with external scoring elements (wires, blades, or ridges) that create controlled micro-incisions in calcified plaque, enabling more effective dilation with lower pressures.

**Key Applications:**
- Calcified lesions
- Resistant lesions
- Pre-stent preparation
- Fibrotic lesions

### 3. Cutting Balloons

**Function:** Balloons with microsurgical blades (atherotomes) that make precise longitudinal incisions in plaque during inflation.

**Key Applications:**
- Severely calcified lesions
- In-stent restenosis
- Ostial lesions
- Bifurcation lesions

## Regulatory Requirements for Georgia Import

### Regulatory Authority

**State Regulation Agency of Medical Activities (RAMA)**
- Operating under: Ministry of Internally Displaced Persons from Occupied Territories, Labour, Health and Social Affairs

### Certification Requirements

Georgian regulations require one of the following certifications:

| Certification | Acceptability | Notes |
|---------------|---------------|-------|
| **CE Mark (MDR)** | ✅ Required | European Medical Device Regulation |
| **FDA Approval** | ✅ Accepted | US Food and Drug Administration |
| **NMPA** | ⚠️ May need additional | Chinese certification alone may require supplementary documentation |

**Additional Requirement:** Products should be mentioned in relevant European clinical guidelines (e.g., ESC guidelines) for enhanced acceptance.

### Required Documentation for Registration

1. Letter on behalf of the firm for representatives
2. Product information and composition
3. Supporting documents showing registration in another country (e.g., Germany)
4. Instructions for use
5. Quality certificates (ISO, EC certificate)
6. Identification number for trade license
7. Production method details
8. Data confirming product is not toxic or mutagenic
9. Original model of marking
10. Release form and structure set
11. Product samples

### Labeling Requirements

**Mandatory Georgian Language Labeling:**
- Device name
- Manufacturer name and address
- Intended use
- Lot/serial number
- Expiry date (if applicable)
- Storage conditions
- Warnings and precautions

### Registration Validity

- **Duration:** 5 years from issuance
- **Renewal:** Application must be submitted at least 2 months before expiration
- **Vigilance:** Manufacturers must establish post-market surveillance system

## Next Steps & Recommendations

### Immediate Actions

1. **Contact Priority Suppliers**
   - Start with Lepu Medical (most comprehensive portfolio, best certifications)
   - Request product catalogs, pricing sheets, and MOQ information
   - Inquire about exclusive distributor opportunities for Georgia

2. **Verify Certifications**
   - Request copies of CE certificates (verify notified body)
   - Confirm MDR compliance status
   - Obtain ISO 13485 certificates

3. **Sample Evaluation**
   - Request product samples for quality assessment
   - Conduct bench testing if possible
   - Consult with local interventional cardiologists

### Medium-Term Actions

4. **Regulatory Preparation**
   - Engage with RAMA for pre-registration consultation
   - Prepare Georgian language labeling requirements
   - Establish post-market surveillance system

5. **Trade Fair Participation**
   - Plan attendance at CMEF Spring 2025 (Shanghai)
   - Schedule manufacturer meetings in advance
   - Evaluate products in person

6. **Negotiate Terms**
   - Discuss pricing tiers based on volume
   - Negotiate payment terms (L/C, T/T)
   - Establish warranty and quality guarantee terms
   - Clarify return/replacement policies

### Due Diligence Checklist

- [ ] Verify manufacturer's physical address and facilities
- [ ] Confirm export licenses and capabilities
- [ ] Check references from other international distributors
- [ ] Review clinical trial data and publications
- [ ] Assess product liability insurance coverage
- [ ] Evaluate technical support capabilities
- [ ] Confirm spare parts and consumables availability
