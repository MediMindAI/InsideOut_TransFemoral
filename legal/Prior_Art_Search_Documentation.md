## 4. DETAILED PATENT SUMMARIES

### 4.1 US9289577B2 - Occlusion Access Method

**Patent Information:**
- **Title:** Occlusion access method
- **Inventors:** John Gurley, Joseph Creagan Trautman, Brice Arnault De La Menardiere, Clayton Baldwin
- **Assignee:** Merit Medical Systems, Inc. (current); Therix Medical Development Ltd (original)
- **Filing Date:** March 13, 2015
- **Issue Date:** March 22, 2016
- **Status:** Active
- **Expiration:** ~2035

**Abstract:**
Method for providing access to a central venous system of a patient using inside-out technique with catheter introduced through femoral vein and navigated to exit point in internal jugular vein region.

**Key Claims Summary:**
- Claim 1: Method for providing access to central VENOUS system
- Claims require radiopaque target application
- Claims specify femoral vein introduction
- Exit point at neck/IJV region

**Relevance to ProGuard™:**
- MEDIUM-HIGH conceptual relevance (inside-out methodology)
- LOW infringement risk (venous-specific claims)
- Distinguishable on anatomical domain (venous vs. arterial)

### 4.2 US9314595B2 - Central Venous Access System

**Patent Information:**
- **Title:** Central venous access system
- **Inventor:** John Gurley
- **Assignee:** University of Kentucky Research Foundation
- **Filing Date:** December 2, 2010
- **Issue Date:** April 19, 2016
- **Status:** Active
- **Expiration:** May 22, 2032

**Abstract:**
Catheter system for accessing central venous system at location near neck, with workstation positioned at IVC/RA junction and needle wire for creating tissue track to external exit target.

**Key Claims Summary:**
- Claim 1: Catheter system for central VENOUS access near neck
- Claims specify: IVC, right atrium, SVC, azygos vein
- Workstation lodged at IVC/RA junction
- Exit location proximate to neck

**Relevance to ProGuard™:**
- MEDIUM conceptual relevance (inside-out concept)
- LOW infringement risk (anatomy-specific claims)
- Distinguishable on anatomical structures and exit location

- **Filing Date:** June 1, 1994
- **Issue Date:** March 25, 1997

**Abstract:**
Improved apparatus and methods for suturing percutaneous luminal puncture sites using guide body with needle guide and contact surface oriented at specific angle.

**Key Claims Summary:**

**Relevance to ProGuard™:**
- LOW relevance (expired; outside-in methodology)
