## KEY FINDINGS

### 1. Freedom to Operate: FAVORABLE

**No blocking patents identified** that would prevent manufacture, sale, import, or use of the ProGuard™ system in target markets (United States, European Union).

### 2. Closest Prior Art: DISTINGUISHABLE

The Merit Medical **Surfacer System** (US9289577B2, US9314595B2) represents the closest prior art with its inside-out access concept, but is **clearly distinguishable** because:

- ❌ Surfacer: **VENOUS** system access
- ✅ ProGuard: **ARTERIAL** system access

This distinction is not semantic—it represents fundamentally different:
- Anatomical targets
- Physiological environments (5-15 mmHg vs 80-120 mmHg)
- Clinical indications (dialysis vs interventional cardiology)
- Patient populations
- Regulatory pathways

### 3. Patentability: STRONG

ProGuard™ demonstrates **strong patentability** across multiple novel features:

| Novel Element | Prior Art Gap |
|---------------|---------------|
| Inside-out **ARTERIAL** access | ✅ No coverage |
| Transradial-to-femoral delivery route | ✅ No coverage |
| Pentad (5-port) needle array | ✅ No coverage |
| Prophylactic suture placement | ✅ No coverage |
| Integrated access + closure device | ✅ No coverage |
